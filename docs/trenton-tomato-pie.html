Trenton tomato pie

7 places for tomato pie, each confirmed in fall 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source. Trenton's style lays the mozzarella on a thin crust first and spreads the crushed tomatoes over it.

Ask for early accessThe free Garden State Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Cheese first, tomatoes on top

Trenton's Chambersburg neighborhood made the tomato pie famous, and Papa's, which opened there in 1912, and De Lorenzo's now both bake in Robbinsville. The crust is thin and crisp, and the tomatoes go on last.

Towns with the most: Robbinsville (2), Jersey City (1), Chesterfield (1), Hainesport (1), Gloucester City (1), Bordentown Township (1).
