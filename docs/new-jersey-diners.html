New Jersey diners

13 diners across New Jersey, the state that calls itself the diner capital of the world, each confirmed in fall 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: vintage dining cars, roadside landmarks and big highway diners.

Built in New Jersey

Many of the country's dining cars were built here: by Jerry O'Mahony in Elizabeth, Paramount in Haledon and later Oakland, Silk City in Paterson and Kullman in Newark and later Lebanon. Some places on this list still serve in those factory-built cars; others are the big stainless diners that replaced them along the highways.

The list at a glance

In 13 towns: Blairstown, Chester Township, Clifton, East Newark, Florham Park, Hammonton, Jefferson, Keyport, Linden, Little Falls, Southampton, Summit and Wall.

Our method: a place made the list only if a dated 2025 or 2026 source named it (local news, a food site or the place's own site), or an older one did and Monmouth's or Camden's county health department inspected it in 2025 or 2026, and its street address matched our records. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.
