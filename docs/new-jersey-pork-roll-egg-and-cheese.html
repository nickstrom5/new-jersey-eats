Pork roll or Taylor ham

John Taylor of Trenton began selling his cured pork roll in 1856, and it's still made in Trenton. Sliced, nicked at the edge so it lies flat, and fried, it goes on a kaiser roll with a fried egg and American cheese, usually with salt, pepper and ketchup.
