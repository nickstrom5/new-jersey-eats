Built around how New Jersey eats

Star ratings won't tell you which diner still runs in a 1940s dining car, or where to get a ripper deep-fried until it splits. Garden State Eats is organized the way people here eat: diners, pork roll, hot dogs, subs and sloppy joes, BYOB restaurants and Shore seafood, each hand-checked place backed by a source from fall 2026. Every place names the health department that inspects its town, and Apple Maps' live card supplies ratings, hours and photos.

Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out in the Pine Barrens.

What stays on your phone

Garden State Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is Garden State Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

The diner, pork roll, tomato pie, hot dog, sub, BYOB and Shore seafood lists are our own research from fall 2026: a dated 2025 or 2026 source names each place (local news, a food site or the place's own site), or an older one does and the county health department inspected the place in 2025 or 2026. The wider directory of 21,754 restaurants comes from Overture Maps' open place data, keeping the kinds of listing that held up best when we checked them.

Is it pork roll or Taylor ham?

Both: it's the same Trenton-made pork product, sliced and fried. North Jersey mostly says Taylor ham and South Jersey says pork roll, and the app finds it either way.

How do I check a New Jersey restaurant's inspection?

Look for the placard at the entrance: Satisfactory, Conditionally Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory, under the state's retail food code. The app shows the latest placard for 1,484 places in Monmouth and Camden counties, whose health departments post ratings online, and names the health department for every other town, since none of the others publishes results online.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
