New Jersey James Beard restaurants

8 New Jersey restaurants named by the James Beard Foundation: finalists and semifinalists from 2024 to 2026, and America's Classics (3 places). New Jersey has no MICHELIN Guide.

What counts as an honor here

One source, reported as facts rather than scores: the James Beard Foundation's Restaurant and Chef Awards for 2024 to 2026 (semifinalists, finalists and winners, each labeled as exactly that) and its America's Classics awards from any year. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place names the chef. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count, and there's no MICHELIN Guide for New Jersey.
